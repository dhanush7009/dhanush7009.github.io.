Privacy policy

This policy covers the Google Drive backup feature of Honda Rider Sync, Money Tracker and Miles & Memories.

What the apps access

The apps use the Google Drive drive.file permission. It allows them to work only with files and folders they created. They cannot see other files in your Drive. The apps read your Google account email address so they can show which account is connected.

What is stored

Backups contain the data you entered in each app, including settings and, for Miles & Memories, your photographs. Backups are encrypted on your phone with a key protected by your recovery password before they are uploaded. Your password is never uploaded. Some details are not encrypted so that backups can be listed: app name, backup date, size and app version.

Where it goes

Backups are stored only in your own Google Drive. The developer runs no server and receives none of your data. There is no advertising or analytics, and data is not sold or shared.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can disconnect an app at any time from its Backup & Restore screen, delete backups in the app or in Google Drive, and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
